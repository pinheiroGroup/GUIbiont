Deterministic:
Stochastic:
Point-by-point — subtracts the mean blank OD at each individual time point. Best choice when blank drifts over time (evaporation, temperature). Recommended default.

Shift minimum — subtracts a single global blank mean, then shifts the entire corrected series up so all values are positive. Preserves growth curve shape. Works well when blank is stable and the well OD is mostly above blank. Can produce a visible discontinuity at the fit start for wells that are mostly below blank.

Clip to zero — subtracts a single global blank mean and clips any negative values to zero. Simple and safe. Some distortion of the early growth phase if many points are negative.
